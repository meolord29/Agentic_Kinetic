Brand mark · the kinetic curve

The drug's own rise and fall after a dose, drawn as one line, with the dot as the next sample. It has no face, and it reads at app-icon size and in a 20px header. In Motion graphics the line traces itself on and the dot lands last.

Gradient mark

The app icon, the header and the celebration moment. The line carries the full cyan → violet → magenta gradient.

Badges · brand ring

The mark's gradient circle and soft inner disc, carrying a tick or the milestone number written in one line. Numbers use the deeper end of the brand colours in light mode, so they stay readable on the disc.

Motion · what moves, and when

Feedback is the default and is what the first build needs: small motion on the thing that just changed. Motion graphics is future development, one arrival sequence per screen that carries the brand, to add only if there is time. Both play once, nothing loops or runs past five seconds (WCAG 2.2.2), and the phone's reduce-motion setting turns all of it off.
