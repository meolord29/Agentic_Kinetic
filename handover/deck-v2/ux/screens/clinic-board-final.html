Agentic Kinetic · Clinic Board · cutting through the noise

A clinic list is mostly noise. Most patients are steady, and the few who need a doctor are buried among them, so every screen asks the doctor to read everything to find one thing. This board does that sorting first, so there is less to hold in your head. The full gradient frame marks the one action to take now, a softer frame marks what can wait until later today, and steady patients fade back. Inside a frame, the bold underlined words are the problem or the action, and numbers outside their range are bold with an arrow, so the eye lands on what matters before reading a sentence. Switch off anything you don't need, colour included. Only Elena's numbers are real backend output; the other patients are placeholders.

      What changed, and why

      Brightness means act

      The full gradient frame is kept for the one thing to act on now. Today's reviews get a soft frame, and steady items go flat and quiet. The tier is also in the words and the dot shape, never in colour alone.

      WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.1 Use of Color; NN/g on preattentive attributes

      The frame points at the words

      Inside a framed card, the exact problem or action is bold and underlined in the frame's own gradient, so the eye goes from the frame to the sentence that matters.

      NN/g on writing for scanning

      Titles say the action

      Every card title is the action in bold, with thin context beside it. Smaller text under the buttons gives the burden and what happens if she waits.

      CDS Hooks card summary; NN/g on progressive disclosure

      Values on their range

      Each number sits on a line with its target band and says where it was measured. Values outside the range are bold, with an arrow and the word, in the one warm colour.

      Zikmund-Fisher et al., JAMIA 2017;24(3):520–528 (number lines vs tables); Few, bullet graphs

      Her clearance against patients like her

      Her estimate and its interval are drawn against the population average and the range 90% of similar patients fall in, which shows why her troughs run low.

      Fernández-Alarcón et al., Front Pharmacol 2024;15:1456565 · de novo population, so an extrapolation for her

      Detail you can turn down

      "Actions and key numbers" hides trend charts, supporting lines, sources, steady patients and the mood module, and leaves the frames, the actions and the numbers that decide them. Colour can be switched off too: every state still reads from words, dot shapes, bold and arrows.

      NN/g on progressive disclosure

      Sources were gathered by research agents. The JAMIA 2017 paper's title, journal and DOI (10.1093/jamia/ocw169) were checked by search, at abstract level. The clearance average and range come from the project's PK module for the demo patient's covariates.
