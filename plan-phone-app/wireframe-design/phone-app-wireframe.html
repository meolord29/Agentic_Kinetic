Agentic Kinetic · patient app · wireframe

A mid-fi wireframe of the patient phone app, end to end: set-up, the daily check-in loop, voice, the booked-sample day and the agentic reminders. Layout and widget anatomy follow the handover contract; the grey-dashed labels and numbered callouts explain what each piece is and where it comes from. Agentic-UI patterns are borrowed from CopilotKit research (see the strip below the flow map): the app is chatless and embedded — the agent decides which widget is big, and never writes the interface itself. Screens marked TODO · proposed go beyond the handover contract; everything else uses the contract copy exactly.

Agentic-UI patterns · CopilotKit inspiration, patient-app translation

Chatless, embedded

CopilotKit: “a dashboard with no conversation anywhere can be deeply agentic… users experience a product feature.”

The patient app has no chat box. The agent speaks only through approved widgets; she answers with one tap or holds one button.

Whole board · AGENTS.md §1

Controlled generative UI

CopilotKit: the agent “chooses which pre-built component fits the moment, never inventing the rendered surface itself.”

The agent decides what is big: due now is one large gradient widget, next is a row, the rest are tiles, done items shrink to a done row. Components and copy are fixed by the contract.

Frame 6 · AGENTS.md §1

Human-in-the-loop card

CopilotKit: Human_Input / Human_Approval cards — a bordered card, the question, discrete answer buttons.

Every question is a card with an eyebrow, one question and closed answers: the four check-in chips, “Yes · No · Not sure” for care-team questions. Identical thanks for every answer.

Frames 5, 8, 12 · AGENTS.md §3.7, §5D, §5F

Tool-call receipt

CopilotKit: “✓ tool.name · TOOL_CALL” rows — each agent action legible, with a checkmark.

Quiet receipt rows under an answer or a handoff: “Sent to your care team · just now.” The thanks line must be literally true of the system.

Frames 8, 12 · proposed copy, TODO

State-delta sync

CopilotKit: “STATE_DELTA · synced” — the interface reflects another actor's change the moment it lands.

“Booked by your care team” appears on her phone only after the clinician accepts on the Clinic Board. A proposal never renders as booked.

Frames 7, 13 · AGENTS.md §5B–C

Provenance label

CopilotKit: “added by agent” tags mark generated content in a native surface.

Every widget carries its source as its eyebrow: “Your care team asks”, “Booked by your care team”, “You asked”. Nothing arrives unlabelled; the menu always discloses “This app uses AI”.

All frames · AGENTS.md §6

Guardrails that shape this wireframe

Patterns, not the pipe. CopilotKit / AG-UI transport belongs to the Clinic Board surface (ARCHITECTURE.md). The patient app never mounts the agent: it reads a plain HTTP route, GET /api/patient/view, and renders the projection only. We borrow the interaction patterns, never the stream.

Never shown on a patient screen: a drug level, a prediction, an interval, in or out of range, direction of travel, dose advice, an interaction verdict, urgency, a countdown, anything about the kidney. No digits in software-written patient text; booked times and her clinician's own words are the exemptions. A health question is never answered — it becomes the handoff.

Out of scope here: the mood / “Hard days” module and Motion graphics are labelled future development in the handover and are not wired or built; authentication is out of scope for the demo backend (no auth by design), so frames 1–4 are proposal only.

Wireframe notes

  • Mid-fi by design: real layout, type and widget anatomy with simplified internals; motion is a build concern (Feedback level only, AGENTS.md §8) and is deliberately absent here.
  • Grounded frames carry their contract reference in the caption; proposal frames are tagged TODO and add nothing to the wire (no fields, no numbers) until the contract is extended.
  • Visual target for the build: handover/deck-v2/ux/screens/patient-widget-final.html — screenshot each finished screen against it and list the differences.
  • Tests that guard this design: AGENTS.md §10 — no clinician field on the patient wire, no digits in software-written patient strings, identical thanks for all four answers, a null next_sample never renders a booking, an unapproved question never renders.
